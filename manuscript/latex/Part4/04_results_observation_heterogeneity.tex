\subsection{Observation Heterogeneity Is Part of the Result}

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/4134_figure5_observation_heterogeneity.png}
\caption{Observation heterogeneity. Route-level evidence and observation
classes show robust survivors, fragile boundaries, and stable failures across
the 19 observations. The metadata association panel is descriptive and
sensitivity-audited, but it is not a causal recording-condition explanation.}
\label{fig:heterogeneity}
\end{figure*}

The cross-observation map showed that heterogeneity was structured rather than
incidental. Thirteen observations were classified as robust survivor/diffuse
positive cases, two as fragile affine-reduction boundaries, two as stable
failures, one as a fragile survivor, and one as a robust survivor without the
diffuse gate (Fig.~\ref{fig:heterogeneity}).

This heterogeneity constrains the manuscript claim. The positive result cannot
be written as a pooled universal effect, and failure observations cannot be
discarded as artifacts without independent evidence. Metadata associations
were therefore treated as descriptive annotations only.
