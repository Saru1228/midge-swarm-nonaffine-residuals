\subsection{Limitations}

Several limitations define how the results should be interpreted. First, the
dataset is observational, so the analysis cannot establish individual-level
causal forces. Second, T1 is a focal-neighborhood aggregate residual rather
than a direct behavioral variable. Third, the 19-observation scope is valuable
for mapping heterogeneity but too small for strong causal metadata
interpretation.

The negative tests are also definition-bound. Failure of the tested
$(C,\dot{C},R)$ moment closure does not rule out higher-dimensional,
delayed, network-based, or observation-specific stochastic descriptions.
Likewise, failure of the state-matched near-pre test does not imply that
transitions have no special dynamics. The propagation route was not
confirmatorily tested and should remain an open future branch.

Finally, the figures in this draft are evidence-panel previews. They are
appropriate for organizing the manuscript logic, but publication graphics will
require a separate design pass.
