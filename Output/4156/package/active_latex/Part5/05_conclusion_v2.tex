\subsection{Conclusion}

This study identified a local tangential non-affine residual that remained
measurable after local affine deformation was removed from laboratory
midge-swarm trajectories. The result is not a complete mechanism: the residual
was common but not universal, was rare under a completed full-pipeline
pseudo-event calibration, passed numerical conditioning checks, remained
partly sensitive to detrending choice, and was not consistently accounted for
by the tested compact-state, event-timing, and recent-history reductions.

Its value is diagnostic. T1 marks a level of collective motion between raw
individual trajectories and low-dimensional swarm state, and it provides a
bounded target for future comparative and mechanistic work.
