\subsection{Conclusion and Future Work}

Taken together, the results indicate that laboratory midge swarms contain a
reproducible local non-affine tangential motion signature that survives local
affine geometric subtraction in most observations, yet resists several natural
low-dimensional reductions and displays explicit observation-level
heterogeneity.

Future work should begin from this bounded observable rather than from an
unqualified search for a universal mechanism. Promising directions include
higher-dimensional path dependence, network-level organization, redesigned
propagation tests, and intervention or perturbation experiments that can
separate descriptive residual structure from causal interaction rules.
