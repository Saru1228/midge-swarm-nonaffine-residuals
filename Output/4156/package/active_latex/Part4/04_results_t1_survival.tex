\subsection{T1 Survives Local Affine Subtraction in Most Observations}

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/4134_figure2_t1_survival_across_observations.png}
\caption{T1 survival across observations. The all-observation panel retains
both survivor and failure observations. T1 survived local affine subtraction in
most observations and remained robust to nearby scale/lag choices inside the
survivor class, but the observation-class panel shows that the result was not
universal.}
\label{fig:t1_survival}
\end{figure*}

The first result is that local affine deformation did not fully absorb the
transition-linked tangential residual in most observations. T1 survived at
least one original local-scale setting in 15 of 19 observations and both
original local-scale settings in 14 of 19 observations
(Fig.~\ref{fig:t1_survival}). Within the survivor class, 14 of 15 observations
were robust to nearby scale and lag choices.

The all-observation display is important for interpretation. Several
observations remained fragile or failed under the predefined criteria. Thus,
the result supports a common local non-affine residual, not a universal T1
law.
