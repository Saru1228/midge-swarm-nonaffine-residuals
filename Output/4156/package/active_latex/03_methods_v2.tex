\section{Methods}

The analysis was organized as a sequence of reduction tests. Each step asked
whether a natural explanation was sufficient before interpreting the remaining
T1 activity as a reproducible local phenomenon. All manuscript-level claims
were evaluated at the observation level rather than by treating all frames or
focal samples as independent observations.
